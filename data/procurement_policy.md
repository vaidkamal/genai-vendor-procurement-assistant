# Bramwell & Co. Consulting — Procurement Policy (extract, fictional)

## P-01 Competitive quotations
Any purchase above USD 25,000 requires a minimum of three written quotations from independent vendors.

## P-02 Budget authority
Purchases must not exceed the approved budget line. Any overrun above 5% requires CFO re-approval before a purchase order is raised.

## P-03 Payment terms
Standard terms are Net 30 or better. Advance payments must not exceed 30% of the order value without Finance Director approval.

## P-04 Warranty and support
IT hardware must carry a minimum three-year manufacturer or vendor warranty. Onsite next-business-day support is preferred for laptops issued to consultants.

## P-05 Information security
Vendors who handle company data or image company devices must hold ISO 27001 (or an equivalent certification accepted by the CISO).

## P-06 Quote validity
Quotations must remain valid for at least 21 days to allow the approval cycle to complete.

## P-07 Human accountability
AI tools may prepare comparisons and recommendations. Only a named procurement officer may approve a vendor, and only Finance may release payment. AI systems must never raise purchase orders or commit funds.

## P-08 Vendor history and references
Previous performance (on-time delivery, defect rate, invoice accuracy, support responsiveness) must be considered for vendors with order history. New vendors require two trade references and a credit check before award.

## P-09 AI voice contact with vendors
An AI voice agent may contact vendors only to clarify quotations. It must identify itself as an AI at the start of the call, state that the call is recorded, and state that it cannot accept offers, place orders or commit funds. Call outcomes are logged for the procurement officer.

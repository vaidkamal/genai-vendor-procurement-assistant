# Demonstration script (≈ 8 minutes)

1. **The problem (1 min).** Open the three PDFs in `data/quotations/`. Point out: USD vs EUR, warranty extension priced separately, freight included or not, 50% deposit, 14-day validity. "A human comparing these makes at least one arithmetic or policy mistake."
2. **Standardise (1 min).** Run `python src/run_pipeline.py`. Open `outputs/standardised_quotations.csv`: one row per vendor, all in USD, all-in totals. Show that Alpine's EUR 1,190 becomes USD 1,377 once the 3-year warranty extension and FX are applied.
3. **Criteria and scores (1.5 min).** Open `app/dashboard.html`. Walk the six weights on the left and say why each weight was chosen (cost capped at 30% so a cheap quote with bad terms cannot win; delivery matters because onboarding is dated; past performance rewards proven vendors). Show the score rows in the comparison table and the formulas in `comparison_report.md`.
4. **Policy (1 min).** Scroll to Policy checks. TechSource passes everything; Alpine is flagged on ISO 27001; MegaByte on the 50% deposit, 14-day validity and no order history. Ask the assistant: "Can we accept a 50% deposit?" (in claude.ai) or run `python src/03_ai_assistant.py --ask ...`.
5. **Clarify (1.5 min).** Open `outputs/clarification_emails.md` (drafts only). Play 30 seconds of `outputs/voice/call_megabyte.mp3`: the agent says it is an AI, the call is recorded, and it cannot commit. Show the outcome table: 30% deposit option, validity extended, onsite warranty priced.
6. **Review and change (1 min).** Move the sliders: raise Technical fit and Delivery, lower Cost — MegaByte overtakes Alpine but TechSource still leads. Reset. Enter your name, choose "Award to a different vendor", leave the justification empty → blocked. Add a justification → recorded in the approval history with the weight snapshot. Export the JSON.
7. **No autonomous purchasing (1 min).** Point to the red guardrail banner. Show `grep -r "smtplib\|requests.post" src/` returns nothing. The only "action" the system takes is writing a JSON instruction that a person in Finance executes. Policy P-07 and P-09 are in `data/procurement_policy.md`.
8. **Metrics and limits (30 s).** README: analyst time per comparison and % of exceptions caught before PO; parser tuned to demo layouts, fixed FX, simulated voice, in-browser audit log — and what production would need.

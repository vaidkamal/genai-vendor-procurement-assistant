## How scores are calculated

- **Cost**: cheapest all-in total ÷ vendor total × 100 (after FX conversion and adding shipping and any warranty extension needed to reach 3 years).
- **Technical fit**: 90 if every mandatory spec is met, 100 if a spec is exceeded; missing specs cap the score at 60.
- **Delivery**: 100 if within the required window, minus 5 points per day late.
- **Warranty**: 70 base for ≥3 years, plus 30 onsite / 15 depot / 10 carry-in; 30 if under 3 years.
- **Commercial**: payment terms (40) + advance payment (30) + quote validity (15) + ISO 27001 (15).
- **Past performance**: on-time delivery ×0.4 + (100 − defect % ×10) ×0.2 + invoice accuracy ×0.2 + support responsiveness ×0.2, minus 10 per open dispute; a vendor with no history scores a neutral 60 and is flagged for reference checks.

_The system never places orders or commits funds. A procurement officer reviews and records the final decision in the dashboard (policy P-07)._
